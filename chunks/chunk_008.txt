Where parts of an arrangement which has a close connection to Australia are executed outside Australia for no apparent business reason, the courts may pay more regard to the aspects of the arrangement which occur in Australia. Some general “rules of thumb” emerge from the case-law, but they must be treated with caution as source must always depend on the particular facts of each case. • Income from services will prima facie have its source where the services are performed: *French (1957) 98 CLR 398, Cam & Sons (1936) 36 SR (NSW) 544, cf Mitchum (1965) 9 AITR 559, Efstathakis (1979) 9 ATR 867. o Note statutory exemptions for some Australian-source income of foreign residents, enacted in Subdiv 842-B of the 1997 Act. Consider why these exemptions may be necessary. • Income from the sale of land will prima facie have its source in the place where the land is located: Rhodesia Metals [1940] 3 ALL ER 422; Thorpe Nominees (1988) 19 ATR 1834. • Income from a scheme of profit-making by purchase and sale of intangible assets such as shares will prima facie depend on the place of contract of purchase and the place of contract of sale: Australian Machinery and Investment Co (1946) 7 ATD 415; 8 ATD 31. • Income from the making of a contract will prima facie have its source where the contract is made: Cliffs International (1985) 16 ATR 601; *United Aircraft Corporation (1943) 68 CLR 525; Murray (1929) 42 CLR 332. • Income by way of interest on a loan will prima facie have its source where the loan contract is made: Philips Gloeilampenfabriken (1954) 10 ATD 435; Spotless Services 95 ATC 4775; Studebaker Corporation (1921) 29 CLR 225. • The source of income from business carried on by a number of activities across different countries – e.g. extraction, processing and sale of minerals - will prima facie be determined by a reasonable apportionment of the income to the activities in each jurisdiction: Kirk [1900] AC 588; Lewis Berger (1927) 39 CLR 468, Murray (1929) 42 CLR 332; W Angliss & Co (1931) 46 CLR 417, Hillsdown Watts (1937) 1 AITR 42. o income from carrying on business through a branch in a country will prima facie have its source in that country. • The source of dividend income is prima facie the source of the profits from which the dividend is paid: International Tax: Principles and Structure LAWS70006 Semester 2, 2026 Subject Outline University of Melbourne Law School - 36 - Nathan; Consolidated Press 2001 ATC 4343; Commissioner of Taxation (NSW) v Freeman (1956) 30 ALJ 42, 48.
