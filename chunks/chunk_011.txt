Div 855 applies to all foreign residents who are individuals, companies and trustees of foreign trusts (s 855-10(1)). Individuals who qualify as temporary residents under s 995-1 are also eligible for the exemption if they satisfy two requirements. The individual must be a temporary resident when the CGT event happened (s 768 -915(a)) and if the individual were a foreign resident when the CGT event happened the gain or loss would have been disregarded under Div 855 (s 768-915(b)). A requirement of the exemption in s 855-10(1)(a) is that a CGT event happens in relation to a CGT asset. For CGT events that do not involve a CGT asset, the CGT event will be treated as applying to the underlying asset (s 855-10(2)). The following legal fictions are created under s 855-10(2) for CGT events that do not involve CGT assets: • CGT event D1 (creating contractual rights) - the CGT asset that is the subject of the creation of the contractual rights, • CGT event D2 (granting an option) - the CGT asset that is the subject of the option, • CGT event F1 (granting a lease) – the CGT asset that is the subject to the lease, • CGT event J1 (a company ceasing to be a member of a wholly-owned group after roll-over) – the roll-over asset. Non-residents and temporary residents are not entitled to the CGT discount.
